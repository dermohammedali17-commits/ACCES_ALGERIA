# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PERPLEXITY RESEARCH PROTOCOL

> Standard research protocol for using Perplexity as an evidence-discovery engine.

---

# 1. PRIMARY ROLE

Perplexity is used primarily for:

- Web research
- Source discovery
- Current information research
- Market intelligence
- Company intelligence
- Regulatory information discovery
- Competitive research

---

# 2. RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Every research request should begin with a clearly defined question.

Avoid broad information collection without a strategic purpose.

---

# 3. SOURCE PRIORITY

Research should prioritize:

1. Official government sources
2. Official company sources
3. Official legal and regulatory sources
4. International institutions
5. Reputable research organizations
6. Established professional publications
7. Specialist industry sources
8. Secondary sources

---

# 4. REQUIRED RESEARCH OUTPUT

Where possible, research results should include:

- Key finding
- Source
- Publication date
- URL
- Relevant quotation or evidence
- Confidence
- Remaining uncertainty

---

# 5. CROSS-CHECKING

Important claims should be cross-checked when:

- The claim materially affects strategy
- The source is secondary
- The information is disputed
- The information is time-sensitive
- The information has significant commercial or regulatory impact

---

# 6. OUTPUT DESTINATION

Validated research should be transferred into the appropriate Access Algeria GitHub section.

Examples:

Market Intelligence
Brand Database
Regulatory Intelligence
Partnership Intelligence
Project Case

---

# 7. AI LIMITATION

A Perplexity-generated answer is not automatically a verified fact.

The underlying sources must be evaluated.
