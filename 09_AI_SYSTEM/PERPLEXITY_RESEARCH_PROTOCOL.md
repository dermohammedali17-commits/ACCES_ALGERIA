# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PERPLEXITY RESEARCH PROTOCOL

## 1. PURPOSE

Perplexity functions as the external research and web-intelligence layer for Access Algeria.

---

## 2. PRIMARY ROLE

Perplexity is primarily used for:

- Current market research
- Regulatory research
- Company research
- Industry intelligence
- Competitor research
- Stakeholder research
- Source discovery
- Evidence collection
- Current-event monitoring

---

## 3. RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

Research should prioritize:

- Official sources
- Government sources
- Company sources
- Regulatory sources
- Primary documentation
- High-quality independent sources

---

## 4. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

Research outputs should clearly identify:

- Claim
- Source
- Publication or update date where available
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Remaining uncertainty

---

## 5. REPOSITORY TRANSFER

Important validated findings should be transformed into structured Access Algeria knowledge.

Research should not remain permanently trapped inside conversations.

---

## 6. FACT VERIFICATION

Important claims should be corroborated when practical.

A single weak source should not automatically become an established Access Algeria fact.

---

## 7. RESEARCH → GITHUB

Validated findings should be transferred into the appropriate system:

Market Intelligence
Brand Database
Partnership Intelligence
Regulatory Intelligence
Opportunity Scoring
Market Entry
Project Knowledge

---

## 8. CORE PRINCIPLE

Perplexity discovers and researches.

Access Algeria's controlled repository stores validated and reusable knowledge.
