# ACCESS ALGERIA
# ANALYSIS TO DECISION WORKFLOW

> Standard workflow for transforming structured intelligence into decision support.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This workflow defines how Access Algeria moves from information to strategic analysis and decision-making.

---

# 2. CORE FLOW

VERIFIED INFORMATION
↓
STRUCTURED INPUT
↓
AI ANALYSIS
↓
STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT
↓
OPTIONS
↓
RISK REVIEW
↓
RECOMMENDATION
↓
HUMAN DECISION
↓
EXECUTION

---

# 3. INPUT REQUIREMENTS

Before requesting strategic analysis, provide:

- The decision question
- Relevant facts
- Sources
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Project objective

---

# 4. ANALYSIS

The AI system should analyze:

- Strategic implications
- Opportunities
- Risks
- Alternative options
- Dependencies
- Information gaps

---

# 5. OPTIONS

Where relevant, the analysis should provide more than one strategic option.

Each option should include:

- Advantages
- Risks
- Requirements
- Expected implications

---

# 6. RECOMMENDATION

A recommendation should clearly explain:

- Why the option is recommended
- What evidence supports it
- What assumptions remain
- What must happen next

---

# 7. HUMAN DECISION

The final decision remains the responsibility of the designated human decision-maker.

Possible decisions:

- APPROVE
- APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS
- RESEARCH FURTHER
- PAUSE
- REJECT

---

# 8. EXECUTION

Approved decisions should be converted into actionable work and transferred to the execution system.
