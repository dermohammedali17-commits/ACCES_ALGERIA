# ACCESS ALGERIA
# AI SYSTEM

> The AI operating layer of Access Algeria.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Access Algeria AI System defines how artificial intelligence tools are used across research, intelligence, analysis, strategic planning and execution.

The purpose is to create a controlled workflow where each AI system has a defined role.

# 3. PRIMARY AI ROLES

## PERPLEXITY

Primary function:

Research and evidence discovery.

---

## CLAUDE

Primary function:

Strategic analysis, synthesis, framework development and decision support.

---

## GEMINI

Secondary function:

Multimodal analysis, alternative research and complementary analytical support.

---

## GITHUB

Primary function:

Knowledge storage, documentation, version control and institutional memory.

---

## MONDAY

Primary function:

Execution, tasks, pipeline management and operational tracking.

---

# 4. CORE PRINCIPLE

No AI system should independently determine a strategic decision without appropriate evidence and human review.
