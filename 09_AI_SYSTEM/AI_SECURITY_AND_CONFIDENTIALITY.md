# AI SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

> Internal principles for responsible use of AI systems.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines basic confidentiality and information-handling principles when using AI systems.

---

# 2. INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

Information should be classified where relevant as:

- PUBLIC
- INTERNAL
- CONFIDENTIAL
- RESTRICTED

---

# 3. PUBLIC INFORMATION

May generally include:

- Public company information
- Public regulations
- Public reports
- Public market information

---

# 4. INTERNAL INFORMATION

Internal working documents and non-public project analysis.

Share only when necessary for the task.

---

# 5. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

May include:

- Commercial discussions
- Partner information
- Strategic negotiations
- Non-public business plans

Confidential information requires careful handling.

---

# 6. RESTRICTED INFORMATION

Highly sensitive information should not be unnecessarily copied into external AI systems.

Access should be limited according to the project's security requirements.

---

# 7. MINIMUM NECESSARY PRINCIPLE

Provide an AI system only with information necessary to complete the required task.

---

# 8. HUMAN REVIEW

Important outputs involving:

- Partnerships
- Commercial commitments
- Legal matters
- Financial matters
- Confidential strategy

should receive appropriate human review.

---

# 9. CORE RULE

Convenience must not override confidentiality.
