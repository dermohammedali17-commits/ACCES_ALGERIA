# ACCESS ALGERIA
# BRAND DATABASE

> A structured intelligence database for international brands and companies with potential relevance to the Algerian market.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Brand Database is designed to identify, organize and monitor international brands and companies that may represent market-entry opportunities in Algeria.

The database supports:

- Market opportunity discovery
- Brand intelligence
- Strategic prioritization
- Opportunity scoring
- Partner identification
- Business development

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

The objective is not to collect every company in the world.

The objective is to build a high-value pipeline of companies with potential strategic relevance to Algeria.

# 4. BRAND DATABASE CATEGORIES

Companies may be classified by:

- Industry
- Sector
- Country
- Market Position
- Company Type
- Algeria Presence
- Regional Presence
- Market-Entry Potential
- Strategic Priority

---

# 5. CORE OUTPUT

Each important brand should eventually have a structured Brand Profile.

The detailed profile structure is maintained within this database.
