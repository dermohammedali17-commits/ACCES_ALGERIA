# ACCESS ALGERIA
# BRAND DATABASE STRUCTURE

> A structured architecture for organizing brand intelligence and market-entry opportunities.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines how brand and company information is organized within the Access Algeria Brand Database.

The objective is to create a scalable system that can manage information about international companies, brands, contacts and market-entry opportunities.

---

# 2. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE

The Brand Database contains five core information layers:

1. Brand Identity
2. Company Intelligence
3. Contact Intelligence
4. Market Opportunity
5. Engagement Status

---

# 3. CORE BRAND RECORD

Each important brand should include:

- Brand Name
- Parent Company
- Country of Origin
- Headquarters
- Industry
- Sector
- Official Website
- International Presence
- Algeria Presence
- Strategic Priority
- Opportunity Status

---

# 4. DATABASE ORGANIZATION

The database should progressively organize information by:

SECTOR
↓
SUB-SECTOR
↓
BRAND
↓
BRAND PROFILE
↓
CONTACT INTELLIGENCE
↓
OPPORTUNITY STATUS

---

# 5. PRIORITY SECTORS

Initial sectors include:

- Automotive
- Luxury
- Tourism
- Hospitality
- Mobility
- International Consumer Brands

Additional sectors may be added when a strategic opportunity is identified.

---

# 6. BRAND RECORD PRINCIPLE

A brand record should not contain unsupported claims.

Important information should reference:

- Source
- Verification Status
- Last Review Date

---

# 7. SCALABILITY RULE

The system should allow Access Algeria to expand from:

10 brands
↓
100 brands
↓
1,000 brands

without changing the fundamental structure.
