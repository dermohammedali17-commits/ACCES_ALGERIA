# ACCESS ALGERIA
# CONTACT INTELLIGENCE

> A structured framework for identifying and managing relevant professional and strategic contact points.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

Contact Intelligence helps Access Algeria identify the right organizations, departments and professional contact points relevant to market-entry opportunities.

The objective is not to collect personal information without purpose.

The objective is to identify appropriate professional channels for strategic engagement.

---

# 2. CONTACT CATEGORIES

Relevant contact categories may include:

- Business Development
- International Markets
- Market Expansion
- Partnerships
- Regional Management
- Sales
- Dealer Development
- Corporate Strategy
- Investment
- Government Relations

---

# 3. CONTACT RECORD

Each relevant contact record may include:

- Organization
- Department
- Professional Role
- Geographic Responsibility
- Official Business Channel
- Source
- Date Verified
- Relationship Status
- Next Action

---

# 4. CONTACT PRIORITY

Contacts may be classified as:

PRIORITY A

Directly relevant to a current strategic opportunity.

PRIORITY B

Potentially relevant for future engagement.

PRIORITY C

General or long-term relevance.

---

# 5. RELATIONSHIP STATUS

- IDENTIFIED
- RESEARCHED
- VERIFIED
- CONTACT PREPARED
- CONTACTED
- RESPONSE RECEIVED
- ACTIVE DISCUSSION
- PAUSED
- CLOSED

---

# 6. CORE RULE

Contact information should only be used for legitimate professional and business purposes.

Access Algeria should prioritize official and professional communication channels.

---

# 7. SECURITY RULE

Do not store:

- Passwords
- Private credentials
- Financial account information
- Sensitive personal information

inside the Access Algeria GitHub repository.
