# ACCESS ALGERIA
# BRAND OPPORTUNITY PIPELINE

> A structured pipeline for moving potential brands from discovery to strategic engagement.

---

# PIPELINE STAGES

## 1. DISCOVERED

A potential brand or company has been identified.

↓

## 2. SCREENING

Initial relevance is assessed.

↓

## 3. RESEARCH

Market, company and strategic information are collected.

↓

## 4. VERIFICATION

Critical information is checked.

↓

## 5. SCORING

The opportunity is evaluated using the Access Algeria Opportunity Scoring System.

↓

## 6. PRIORITIZATION

The opportunity is classified according to strategic importance.

↓

## 7. STRATEGY DEVELOPMENT

A potential Algeria market-entry approach is analyzed.

↓

## 8. ENGAGEMENT PREPARATION

Access Algeria prepares:

- Value proposition
- Strategic message
- Relevant intelligence
- Partnership concepts

↓

## 9. ACTIVE ENGAGEMENT

Communication or meetings with relevant stakeholders.

↓

## 10. ACTIVE PROJECT

A structured market-entry project is developed.

↓

## 11. PAUSED OR CLOSED

The opportunity is not currently active.

---

# PIPELINE RULE

An opportunity may move:

FORWARD
↓
BACKWARD
↓
TO WATCHLIST
↓
TO PAUSED
↓
TO CLOSED

Movement between stages must be based on available evidence and strategic decisions.

---

# CORE PIPELINE PRINCIPLE

DISCOVER

is not equal to

OPPORTUNITY

An identified company becomes a strategic opportunity only after sufficient assessment.
