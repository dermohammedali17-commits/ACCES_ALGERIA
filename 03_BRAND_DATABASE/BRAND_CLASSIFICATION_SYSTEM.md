# ACCESS ALGERIA
# BRAND CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

> A structured classification system for organizing international brands and companies.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This system creates a consistent method for classifying companies within the Access Algeria Brand Database.

---

# 2. SECTOR CLASSIFICATION

Each company should be classified by:

- Primary Sector
- Sub-Sector
- Industry

Example:

AUTOMOTIVE
↓
Luxury Automotive
↓
Performance Vehicles

---

# 3. MARKET POSITION

Possible classifications include:

- Mass Market
- Value
- Mid-Market
- Premium
- Luxury
- Ultra-Luxury

---

# 4. ALGERIA PRESENCE

Classify as:

- NO CONFIRMED PRESENCE
- INDIRECT PRESENCE
- LOCAL PARTNER
- DISTRIBUTOR
- DIRECT PRESENCE
- UNDER REVIEW

---

# 5. OPPORTUNITY STATUS

- WATCHLIST
- RESEARCH CANDIDATE
- UNDER RESEARCH
- VERIFIED OPPORTUNITY
- PRIORITY OPPORTUNITY
- ACTIVE ENGAGEMENT
- PAUSED
- CLOSED

---

# 6. STRATEGIC PRIORITY

## PRIORITY A

High strategic relevance and strong opportunity potential.

## PRIORITY B

Relevant opportunity requiring further validation.

## PRIORITY C

Long-term or lower-priority opportunity.

---

# 7. CORE RULE

Classification should be based on evidence.

A company should not be classified as a high-priority opportunity without appropriate research and assessment.
