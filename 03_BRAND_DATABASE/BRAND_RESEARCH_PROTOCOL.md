# ACCESS ALGERIA
# BRAND RESEARCH PROTOCOL

> A standard research process for evaluating international brands and companies.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This protocol defines the standard process for researching a potential brand or company before it is evaluated as a market-entry opportunity.

---

# 2. RESEARCH PROCESS

STEP 1 — IDENTIFY

Identify the company or brand.

↓

STEP 2 — BASIC PROFILE

Collect:

- Company identity
- Country of origin
- Headquarters
- Industry
- Core products or services

↓

STEP 3 — INTERNATIONAL PRESENCE

Analyze:

- Key markets
- International expansion
- Regional presence
- Middle East presence
- Africa presence

↓

STEP 4 — ALGERIA STATUS

Investigate:

- Existing presence
- Distributor
- Local partner
- Official representation
- Market absence

↓

STEP 5 — MARKET ENTRY SIGNALS

Identify potential signals such as:

- International expansion
- New market activity
- Partnership announcements
- Distributor development
- Regional growth

↓

STEP 6 — CONTACT RESEARCH

Identify appropriate:

- Departments
- Official channels
- Business development functions
- Regional management functions

↓

STEP 7 — INITIAL OPPORTUNITY ASSESSMENT

Evaluate:

- Algeria relevance
- Strategic fit
- Potential demand
- Entry barriers
- Partnership potential

↓

STEP 8 — VERIFICATION

Verify critical claims before strategic use.

---

# 3. RESEARCH OUTPUT

Every important research process should produce:

- Brand Profile
- Source Record
- Algeria Status
- Initial Opportunity Assessment
- Recommended Next Action

---

# 4. CORE PRINCIPLE

Research should answer a strategic question.

Information collection without a strategic purpose should be avoided.

---

# 5. FINAL RESEARCH QUESTION

The central question is:

> Is there a credible and strategically valuable market-entry opportunity for this company in Algeria?
