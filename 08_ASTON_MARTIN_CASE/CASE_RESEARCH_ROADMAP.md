# ACCESS ALGERIA
# ASTON MARTIN CASE RESEARCH ROADMAP

> A structured research roadmap for the Aston Martin Algeria market-entry case.

---

# PHASE 1 — BRAND INTELLIGENCE

Research:

- Brand positioning
- International presence
- Regional strategy
- Existing market-entry models
- Expansion indicators

---

# PHASE 2 — ALGERIA MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Research:

- Luxury automotive demand
- Target customer segments
- Purchasing environment
- Competition
- Relevant market trends

---

# PHASE 3 — REGULATORY INTELLIGENCE

Research:

- Investment requirements
- Automotive sector requirements
- Import requirements
- Distribution requirements
- Relevant institutional procedures

---

# PHASE 4 — PARTNERSHIP INTELLIGENCE

Research:

- Potential investors
- Potential automotive operators
- Potential strategic partners
- Operational ecosystem

---

# PHASE 5 — OPPORTUNITY ASSESSMENT

Evaluate:

- Market attractiveness
- Brand-market fit
- Demand potential
- Regulatory feasibility
- Partner availability
- Economic potential

---

# PHASE 6 — MARKET ENTRY ARCHITECTURE

Develop:

- Entry options
- Partner strategy
- Recommended structure
- Implementation roadmap

---

# RESEARCH PRIORITY RULE

Research should begin with questions that have the greatest impact on the strategic decision.
