# 2. INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

## VERIFIED FACT

Information supported by reliable and relevant evidence.

---

## SOURCED INFORMATION

Information from a documented source that may require additional verification.

---

## ASSUMPTION

A statement used for analysis but not yet sufficiently verified.

---

## STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS

A possible explanation or opportunity that requires testing.
