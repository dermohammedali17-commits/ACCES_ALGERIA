# ACCESS ALGERIA
# OPERATIONAL KPI FRAMEWORK

> Framework for measuring the operational performance of Access Algeria.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

KPIs should measure whether Access Algeria is converting intelligence and opportunities into meaningful strategic and commercial outcomes.

---

## 2. OPPORTUNITY KPIs

Track:

- Opportunities Identified
- Opportunities Qualified
- Opportunities Activated
- Opportunities Rejected
- Opportunity Conversion Rate

---

## 3. RESEARCH KPIs

Track:

- Research Tasks Completed
- Verification Completion Rate
- Research Turnaround Time
- Critical Evidence Identified

---

## 4. STAKEHOLDER KPIs

Track:

- New Strategic Contacts
- Qualified Stakeholders
- Meetings
- Follow-Ups
- Active Relationships
- Partnership Opportunities

---

## 5. PROJECT KPIs

Track:

- Active Projects
- Milestone Completion
- Tasks Completed
- Delayed Tasks
- Project Risks
- Project Outcomes

---

## 6. MARKET ENTRY KPIs

Track:

- Brands Assessed
- Market Entry Opportunities
- Feasibility Assessments
- Market Entry Projects
- Active Negotiations
- Successful Market Entry Outcomes

---

## 7. STRATEGIC KPIs

Track:

- Strategic Decisions
- Decision-to-Action Rate
- Opportunity Conversion
- Partnership Conversion
- Project Success Rate

---

## 8. KPI PRINCIPLE

KPIs should support decision-making.

Avoid measuring activity simply for the purpose of producing numbers.

---

## 9. REVIEW FREQUENCY

Operational KPIs may be reviewed:

- Weekly for execution
- Monthly for performance
- Quarterly for strategic assessment

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Measure what contributes to Access Algeria's strategic objectives, not merely what is easy to count.
