# ACCESS ALGERIA
# RESEARCH WORKFLOW

> Standard operational workflow for conducting and documenting Access Algeria research.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This workflow defines how research requests are converted into structured and usable intelligence.

---

# 2. RESEARCH REQUEST

Every research task must define:

- Question
- Objective
- Project
- Required Output
- Deadline
- Priority

---

# 3. RESEARCH PROCESS

DEFINE QUESTION
↓
IDENTIFY SOURCES
↓
COLLECT INFORMATION
↓
VERIFY
↓
ANALYZE
↓
DOCUMENT
↓
STORE
↓
COMMUNICATE RESULT

---

# 4. RESEARCH PRIORITIES

Priority should generally be given to:

1. Primary sources
2. Official sources
3. Current sources
4. Relevant sources
5. Independent corroboration

---

# 5. RESEARCH OUTPUT

The final research output should distinguish:

- Facts
- Assumptions
- Analysis
- Uncertainty
- Recommendations

---

# 6. STORAGE

Research should be stored in the appropriate Access Algeria system.

Examples:

Market Research
→ Market Intelligence

Brand Research
→ Brand Database

Regulatory Research
→ Regulatory Intelligence

Partner Research
→ Partnership Intelligence

---

# 7. COMPLETION CRITERIA

A research task is complete when:

- The research question is answered
- Major sources are documented
- Important claims are appropriately verified
- Remaining uncertainty is identified
- The output is stored in the correct location

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Research is successful when it improves a decision, not merely when information has been collected.
