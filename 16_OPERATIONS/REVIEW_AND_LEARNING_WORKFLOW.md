# ACCESS ALGERIA
# REVIEW AND LEARNING WORKFLOW

> Standard process for reviewing results and converting experience into organizational learning.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

Access Algeria should continuously improve its methods based on real-world results.

---

## 2. REVIEW TRIGGERS

Reviews may occur:

- Weekly
- Monthly
- At major milestones
- After important meetings
- After project completion
- After major strategic changes

---

## 3. REVIEW PROCESS

RESULTS
↓
COMPARE WITH OBJECTIVES
↓
IDENTIFY VARIANCES
↓
ANALYZE CAUSES
↓
CAPTURE LESSONS
↓
UPDATE SYSTEM
↓
IMPROVE FUTURE ACTION

---

## 4. REVIEW QUESTIONS

- What happened?
- What was expected?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- Why?
- What evidence changed?
- What should be repeated?
- What should be avoided?
- What should be changed?

---

## 5. KNOWLEDGE UPDATE

Lessons may update:

- Methodologies
- Templates
- Research protocols
- Scoring criteria
- Strategic assumptions
- Operational workflows

---

## 6. PROJECT LEARNING

Every completed major project should generate:

- Results Summary
- Lessons Learned
- Key Decisions
- Recommendations
- Knowledge Updates

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Experience becomes an organizational asset only when it is captured, structured and reused.
