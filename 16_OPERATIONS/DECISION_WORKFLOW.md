# ACCESS ALGERIA
# DECISION WORKFLOW

> Standard process for converting evidence and analysis into strategic decisions.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Decision Workflow ensures that important Access Algeria decisions are structured, evidence-based and traceable.

---

## 2. DECISION INPUTS

A decision may use:

- Market Intelligence
- Brand Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Partnership Intelligence
- Opportunity Score
- Financial Analysis
- Stakeholder Feedback
- Strategic Objectives

---

## 3. DECISION PROCESS

QUESTION
↓
EVIDENCE
↓
OPTIONS
↓
ANALYSIS
↓
RISKS
↓
RECOMMENDATION
↓
DECISION
↓
ACTION
↓
REVIEW

---

## 4. DECISION RECORD

Important decisions should include:

- Decision
- Date
- Context
- Evidence
- Options Considered
- Rationale
- Risks
- Expected Outcome
- Owner
- Next Action

---

## 5. DECISION TYPES

- Strategic
- Commercial
- Operational
- Partnership
- Market Entry
- Regulatory
- Investment
- Project

---

## 6. DECISION STATUS

- PROPOSED
- UNDER REVIEW
- APPROVED
- REJECTED
- DEFERRED
- SUPERSEDED

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Important decisions should be traceable from evidence to action.
