# ACCESS ALGERIA
# OPPORTUNITY ACTIVATION WORKFLOW

> Standard process for converting a qualified opportunity into an active Access Algeria initiative.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

An opportunity should become an active initiative only when sufficient information and strategic justification exist.

---

# 2. ACTIVATION CONDITIONS

Consider activation when:

- Strategic relevance is established
- Initial research is sufficient
- Opportunity potential is credible
- Required resources are available
- A clear next objective exists

---

# 3. ACTIVATION PROCESS

QUALIFIED OPPORTUNITY
↓
STRATEGIC REVIEW
↓
ACTIVATION DECISION
↓
PROJECT CREATION
↓
OWNER ASSIGNMENT
↓
MILESTONES
↓
MONDAY EXECUTION

---

# 4. ACTIVATION DECISION

Possible decisions:

- ACTIVATE
- RESEARCH FURTHER
- WATCHLIST
- PAUSE
- CLOSE

---

# 5. PROJECT CREATION

An activated opportunity should receive:

- Project Name
- Project Owner
- Strategic Objective
- Scope
- Expected Outcome
- Major Workstreams
- Initial Milestones
- Required Resources

---

# 6. SYSTEM LINKS

An activated opportunity should connect to relevant:

- Brand Record
- Opportunity Score
- Market Intelligence
- Regulatory Assessment
- Partner Strategy
- Market Entry Strategy

---

# 7. EXECUTION TRANSFER

Approved project actions should be transferred to:

10_MONDAY_EXECUTION_SYSTEM

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Do not activate an opportunity simply because it is interesting.

Activate it because there is a justified strategic reason to invest resources.
