# ACCESS ALGERIA
# STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT WORKFLOW

> Standard workflow for managing interactions with brands, partners, institutions and other stakeholders.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This workflow defines how Access Algeria prepares, initiates, manages and follows up stakeholder relationships.

---

## 2. STAKEHOLDER TYPES

- International Brands
- Manufacturers
- Local Companies
- Strategic Partners
- Investors
- Financial Institutions
- Government Institutions
- Business Organizations
- Incubators
- Industry Experts

---

## 3. ENGAGEMENT PROCESS

IDENTIFY
↓
RESEARCH
↓
QUALIFY
↓
PREPARE
↓
CONTACT
↓
ENGAGE
↓
FOLLOW UP
↓
NEGOTIATE
↓
DECIDE
↓
RECORD

---

## 4. BEFORE CONTACT

Confirm:

- Stakeholder identity
- Strategic relevance
- Decision-maker
- Contact information
- Engagement objective
- Value proposition
- Relevant supporting evidence

---

## 5. ENGAGEMENT RECORD

Record:

- Stakeholder
- Organization
- Role
- Date
- Channel
- Objective
- Outcome
- Next Action
- Responsible Person

---

## 6. FOLLOW-UP

Every meaningful interaction should have:

- Next action
- Responsible owner
- Target date
- Current status

---

## 7. B2B AND B2G

Access Algeria may operate through:

### B2B

Engagement with:

- International companies
- Manufacturers
- Local businesses
- Investors
- Strategic partners

### B2G

Engagement with:

- Government institutions
- Public organizations
- Investment-support bodies
- Relevant administrative authorities

B2B and B2G activities must remain clearly documented and governed.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Relationship management should be treated as a strategic asset, not as informal communication.
