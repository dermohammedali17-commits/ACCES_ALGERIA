# ACCESS ALGERIA
# OPPORTUNITY INTAKE WORKFLOW

> Standard process for receiving, registering and initially evaluating a new market-entry opportunity.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Opportunity Intake Workflow defines how Access Algeria handles a newly identified company, brand, project or market-entry opportunity.

The objective is to ensure that every opportunity enters the system in a consistent and traceable manner.

---

# 2. OPPORTUNITY SOURCES

An opportunity may originate from:

- International brand research
- Direct company contact
- Existing relationships
- Local partner referrals
- Market intelligence
- Industry events
- Investment activity
- Institutional or ecosystem signals

---

# 3. INTAKE PROCESS

OPPORTUNITY IDENTIFIED
↓
INITIAL RECORD
↓
BASIC INFORMATION
↓
PRELIMINARY SCREENING
↓
RESEARCH DECISION
↓
PIPELINE REGISTRATION

---

# 4. INITIAL RECORD

Record:

- Company / Brand
- Sector
- Country
- Opportunity Description
- Source
- Date Identified
- Initial Strategic Rationale

---

# 5. PRELIMINARY SCREENING

Assess:

- Algeria relevance
- Sector relevance
- Potential market demand
- Strategic fit
- Initial feasibility
- Potential commercial value

---

# 6. SCREENING DECISION

Possible outcomes:

- RESEARCH
- WATCHLIST
- REJECT

---

# 7. REQUIRED RECORDS

A qualified opportunity should be linked to:

- Brand Database
- Market Intelligence
- Opportunity Scoring
- Relevant Project

---

# 8. CORE PRINCIPLE

Every opportunity should have:

- A clear identity
- A clear source
- A clear reason for consideration
- A defined next action
