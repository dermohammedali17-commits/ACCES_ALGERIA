# ACCESS ALGERIA
# MARKET ENTRY STAGES

> Standard stages for structuring an international company's entry into Algeria.

---

# STAGE 1 — MARKET DISCOVERY

## Objective

Determine whether Algeria should receive strategic attention.

## Key Outputs

- Initial Market Profile
- Initial Opportunity Hypothesis
- Initial Risk View

---

# STAGE 2 — MARKET VALIDATION

## Objective

Determine whether a credible commercial opportunity exists.

## Key Outputs

- Market Assessment
- Customer Analysis
- Competitive Assessment
- Demand Assessment

---

# STAGE 3 — ENTRY FEASIBILITY

## Objective

Determine whether the opportunity is realistically executable.

## Key Outputs

- Regulatory Assessment
- Operational Assessment
- Investment Requirements
- Entry Barriers

---

# STAGE 4 — ENTRY MODEL SELECTION

## Objective

Determine the most appropriate market-entry structure.

Potential models may include:

- Distributor
- Commercial Agency
- Strategic Partnership
- Joint Venture
- Direct Investment
- Licensing
- Franchise
- Other legally viable structures

---

# STAGE 5 — PARTNER STRATEGY

## Objective

Identify and evaluate potential local partners.

## Key Outputs

- Partner Map
- Partner Shortlist
- Partner Evaluation
- Engagement Strategy

---

# STAGE 6 — COMMERCIAL STRUCTURING

## Objective

Transform the opportunity into a defined commercial project.

## Key Outputs

- Roles
- Responsibilities
- Commercial Model
- Investment Structure
- Milestones
- Risk Allocation

---

# STAGE 7 — MARKET ENTRY PREPARATION

## Objective

Prepare the organization for launch.

Potential areas:

- Operations
- Facilities
- Staffing
- Supply Chain
- Distribution
- Customer Support
- Marketing

---

# STAGE 8 — MARKET ENTRY

## Objective

Execute the approved market-entry plan.

---

# STAGE 9 — MARKET DEVELOPMENT

## Objective

Develop and optimize the local market presence.

Potential areas:

- Sales
- Partnerships
- Customer Development
- Brand Development
- Expansion

---

# STAGE 10 — REVIEW

## Objective

Measure outcomes and improve the market-entry model.

---

# DECISION PRINCIPLE

Progression between stages requires sufficient evidence and readiness.

An opportunity may be:

- Advanced
- Paused
- Returned for further analysis
- Rejected
