# A. MARKET

- [ ] Market size assessed
- [ ] Market growth assessed
- [ ] Target customers identified
- [ ] Demand indicators assessed
- [ ] Competitive environment assessed

---

# B. BRAND

- [ ] Brand strategy understood
- [ ] International expansion strategy reviewed
- [ ] Regional presence reviewed
- [ ] Algeria relevance assessed

---

# C. REGULATORY

- [ ] Relevant regulations identified
- [ ] Official sources reviewed
- [ ] Required approvals identified
- [ ] Entry constraints identified
- [ ] Legal review obtained where required

---

# D. COMMERCIAL

- [ ] Business model defined
- [ ] Revenue potential assessed
- [ ] Pricing environment assessed
- [ ] Investment requirements assessed
- [ ] Cost structure assessed

---

# E. PARTNERS

- [ ] Partner ecosystem mapped
- [ ] Potential partners identified
- [ ] Partner shortlist developed
- [ ] Partner due diligence initiated
- [ ] Engagement strategy prepared

---

# F. OPERATIONS

- [ ] Operational requirements identified
- [ ] Supply chain considered
- [ ] Facilities considered
- [ ] Staffing requirements considered
- [ ] After-sales or support requirements considered where relevant

---

# G. STRATEGIC READINESS

- [ ] Opportunity score completed
- [ ] Key assumptions validated
- [ ] Key risks identified
- [ ] Market-entry model selected
- [ ] Implementation roadmap developed
