# ACCESS ALGERIA
# CHANGE MANAGEMENT

> Defines how important changes are managed within the repository.

---

## 1. CHANGE TYPES

Changes may include:

- New Information
- New Evidence
- Strategy Updates
- Market Changes
- Regulatory Changes
- Methodology Improvements
- Structural Corrections

---

## 2. BEFORE MAKING A MAJOR CHANGE

Consider:

- Why is the change necessary?
- What evidence supports it?
- Which systems are affected?
- Does it create duplication?
- Does it require a decision record?

---

## 3. UPDATE PRINCIPLE

When important information changes:

1. Update the primary source document
2. Review connected systems
3. Update affected dashboards
4. Record significant decisions when necessary

---

## 4. VERSION AWARENESS

Major documents should maintain:

- Version
- Status
- Document Owner

---

## 5. CHANGE LOGIC

NEW EVIDENCE
↓
VERIFICATION
↓
ANALYSIS
↓
DECISION
↓
SYSTEM UPDATE
↓
EXECUTION UPDATE

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Do not change strategic information without understanding its impact on the wider system.
