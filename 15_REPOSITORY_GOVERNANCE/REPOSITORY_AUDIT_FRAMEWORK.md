# ACCESS ALGERIA
# REPOSITORY AUDIT FRAMEWORK

> Framework for reviewing the quality, completeness and structure of the Access Algeria repository.

---

## 1. STRUCTURE AUDIT

Check:

- Are all required systems present?
- Are folder names correct?
- Are files located in the correct system?
- Are there duplicate folders?
- Are there unnecessary files?

---

## 2. CONTENT AUDIT

Check:

- Does each file have a clear purpose?
- Is the content complete?
- Is information duplicated?
- Are important areas missing?

---

## 3. GOVERNANCE AUDIT

Check:

- Are important claims sourced?
- Are assumptions identified?
- Are strategic decisions recorded?
- Are verification standards followed?

---

## 4. SYSTEM INTEGRATION AUDIT

Check:

- Are systems connected?
- Is information flowing correctly?
- Are decisions connected to execution?
- Are dashboards connected to source systems?

---

## 5. REAL-WORLD TEST

Test the system using a real project.

Initial validation project:

Aston Martin Algeria

---

## 6. AUDIT RESULTS

Each finding should be classified as:

- COMPLETE
- NEEDS IMPROVEMENT
- MISSING
- DUPLICATED
- INCORRECTLY LOCATED
- ARCHIVE / REMOVE

---

## 7. AUDIT PROCESS

REVIEW
↓
IDENTIFY
↓
CLASSIFY
↓
CORRECT
↓
VERIFY
↓
INTEGRATE
↓
TEST

---

## 8. FINAL OBJECTIVE

The objective is not to have the largest repository.

The objective is to have a reliable operating system that supports real strategic and commercial work.
