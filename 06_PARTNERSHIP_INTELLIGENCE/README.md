# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PARTNERSHIP INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM

> A structured system for identifying, analyzing and developing strategic partnerships relevant to market entry into Algeria.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Partnership Intelligence System helps Access Algeria identify and evaluate organizations that may contribute to successful market entry.

Potential partners may include:

- Investors
- Local companies
- Distributors
- Strategic partners
- Service providers
- Industry operators
- Institutional stakeholders

---

# 2. CORE QUESTION

The system answers:

> Who are the most relevant partners for a specific market-entry opportunity?

# 4. CORE PRINCIPLE

A large company is not automatically the right partner.

The best partner is the organization with the strongest alignment between:

- Strategic capability
- Market position
- Financial capacity
- Operational capability
- Relationship potential
- Project fit

---

# 5. SYSTEM OUTPUTS

The system may produce:

- Partner Ecosystem Map
- Partner Profiles
- Partner Shortlists
- Partner Assessments
- Partnership Strategy
- Engagement Plans
