# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PARTNER CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

> A structured classification framework for organizing potential partners.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This framework provides a consistent method for classifying organizations within the Access Algeria partnership ecosystem.

---

# 2. PARTNER TYPE

Potential categories include:

- Investor
- Corporate Partner
- Distributor
- Commercial Partner
- Strategic Partner
- Operational Partner
- Technology Partner
- Service Provider

---

# 3. STRATEGIC LEVEL

## LEVEL A — STRATEGIC

High strategic importance and major potential contribution.

## LEVEL B — DEVELOPMENT

Relevant partner with significant development potential.

## LEVEL C — SUPPORTING

Useful supporting capability.

## WATCHLIST

Potential future relevance.

---

# 4. RELATIONSHIP STATUS

- PROSPECT
- RESEARCH
- VERIFIED
- PRIORITY
- CONTACTED
- ENGAGED
- ACTIVE
- PAUSED
- CLOSED

---

# 5. PROJECT RELEVANCE

A partner may be relevant to:

- Specific Brand
- Specific Sector
- Specific Market Entry Project
- Multiple Projects
- Long-Term Ecosystem Development

---

# 6. CORE RULE

Classification must be based on evidence and strategic relevance.
