# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PARTNER EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

> A structured framework for evaluating the suitability of potential strategic partners.

---

# 1. EVALUATION DIMENSIONS

Each potential partner should be evaluated according to:

- Strategic Fit
- Financial Capacity
- Operational Capability
- Industry Experience
- Market Access
- Reputation
- Project Alignment
- Long-Term Potential

---

# 2. EVALUATION PROCESS

IDENTIFY
↓
RESEARCH
↓
VERIFY
↓
ASSESS
↓
COMPARE
↓
PRIORITIZE
↓
ENGAGE

---

# 3. KEY QUESTIONS

## Strategic Fit

Does the organization align with the project?

## Financial Capacity

Does the organization appear capable of supporting the required investment?

## Operational Capability

Does the organization have the necessary infrastructure and execution capability?

## Market Access

Does the organization provide relevant access to customers, networks or capabilities?

## Project Alignment

Does the partner fit the specific opportunity?

---

# 4. EVALUATION OUTPUT

Each assessment should produce:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Risks
- Strategic Value
- Recommended Role
- Priority Level
- Recommended Action

---

# 5. CORE PRINCIPLE

The objective is not to find the largest partner.

The objective is to identify the most suitable partner for the specific opportunity.
