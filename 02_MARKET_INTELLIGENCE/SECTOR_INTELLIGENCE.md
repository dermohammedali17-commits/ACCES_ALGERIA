# ACCESS ALGERIA
# SECTOR INTELLIGENCE

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework organizes intelligence related to priority sectors in Algeria.

---

# 2. PRIORITY SECTORS

Initial sectors include:

- Automotive
- Luxury
- Tourism
- Hospitality
- Mobility
- International Brands

Additional sectors may be added based on strategic opportunity.

---

# 3. SECTOR ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

Each sector should be analyzed according to:

- Market Size
- Growth
- Demand
- Customer Segments
- Competition
- Regulation
- Investment Activity
- Key Companies
- Market Gaps
- Entry Barriers
- Strategic Opportunities

---

# 4. SECTOR INTELLIGENCE STRUCTURE

Each major sector may later have its own folder.

Example:

AUTOMOTIVE
↓
Market Overview
Competition
Regulation
Brands
Partners
Opportunities

---

# 5. CORE QUESTION

For every sector, Access Algeria should ask:

> Where does the real market opportunity exist?

Not:

> What information can we collect?
