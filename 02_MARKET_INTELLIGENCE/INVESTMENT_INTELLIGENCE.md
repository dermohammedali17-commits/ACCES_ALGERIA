# ACCESS ALGERIA
# INVESTMENT INTELLIGENCE

> Monitoring investment conditions, capital activity and strategic investment opportunities in Algeria.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document organizes investment intelligence relevant to international companies, investors and market-entry projects.

---

# 2. CORE INVESTMENT AREAS

Monitor:

- Foreign Direct Investment
- Strategic sectors
- Major investment projects
- Public investment priorities
- Private investment activity
- Financing environment
- Investment incentives
- Investment constraints

---

# 3. INVESTMENT INTELLIGENCE QUESTIONS

- Which sectors are attracting investment?
- Which projects are entering the market?
- Where is capital being deployed?
- What incentives may exist?
- What barriers may investors face?
- Which strategic partners have investment capacity?

---

# 4. INVESTMENT RECORD STANDARD

Important investment information should include:

- Investment Activity
- Organization
- Sector
- Estimated Value
- Date
- Source
- Strategic Relevance
- Verification Status

---

# 5. STRATEGIC INTERPRETATION

Investment intelligence should help identify:

- Growing sectors
- Investor activity
- Potential partners
- Competitive signals
- Market-entry opportunities

---

# 6. CORE PRINCIPLE

Investment announcements are not automatically completed investments.

The status of an investment should be clearly identified as:

- Announced
- Planned
- Approved
- Under Development
- Operational
