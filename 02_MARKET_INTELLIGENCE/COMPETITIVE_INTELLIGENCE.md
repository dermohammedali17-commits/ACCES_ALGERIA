# ACCESS ALGERIA
# COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE

> Understanding competitive structures, market positions and strategic gaps in Algeria.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document provides a framework for analyzing competition relevant to market-entry opportunities.

The objective is not simply to list competitors.

The objective is to understand:

- Market structure
- Competitive positioning
- Strategic gaps
- Entry barriers
- Unserved opportunities

---

# 2. COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS AREAS

For each relevant market, analyze:

- Existing competitors
- Market leaders
- International brands
- Local companies
- Distribution structures
- Customer segments
- Pricing environment
- Market positioning

---

# 3. KEY QUESTIONS

- Who currently serves the market?
- Who controls key distribution channels?
- Which customer segments are underserved?
- Which international brands are absent?
- Where are the strategic gaps?
- What makes market entry difficult?

---

# 4. COMPETITIVE RECORD

Each important competitor should be assessed according to:

- Company or Brand
- Market Position
- Target Segment
- Key Strengths
- Potential Weaknesses
- Market Presence
- Strategic Threat Level

---

# 5. COMPETITIVE GAP PRINCIPLE

The strongest opportunity may exist where:

MARKET DEMAND
+
LIMITED COMPETITION
+
FEASIBLE MARKET ENTRY

=

STRATEGIC OPPORTUNITY

---

# 6. STRATEGIC OUTPUT

Competitive intelligence should produce one or more of the following:

- Competitive Map
- Gap Analysis
- Positioning Assessment
- Entry Barrier Assessment
- Opportunity Identification
