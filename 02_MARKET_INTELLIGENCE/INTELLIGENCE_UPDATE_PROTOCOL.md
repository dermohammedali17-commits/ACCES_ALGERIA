# ACCESS ALGERIA
# INTELLIGENCE UPDATE PROTOCOL

> A standard process for maintaining current and reliable market intelligence.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This protocol defines how Access Algeria reviews and updates strategic intelligence.

---

# 2. UPDATE FREQUENCY

## HIGH-CHANGE INFORMATION

Review frequently:

- Regulations
- Government decisions
- Market-entry requirements
- Company announcements
- Major investments

## MEDIUM-CHANGE INFORMATION

Review periodically:

- Competition
- Market trends
- Investment activity
- Partnership landscape

## LOW-CHANGE INFORMATION

Review when necessary:

- Geography
- Long-term economic structure
- Historical information

---

# 3. UPDATE TRIGGERS

Information should be reviewed when:

- A major regulation changes
- A new official announcement is released
- A company enters or exits the market
- A major investment is announced
- A strategic decision is approaching

---

# 4. UPDATE PROCESS

IDENTIFY OUTDATED INFORMATION
↓
LOCATE CURRENT SOURCES
↓
VERIFY CHANGES
↓
UPDATE RECORD
↓
REVIEW STRATEGIC IMPACT
↓
DOCUMENT UPDATE

---

# 5. CHANGE RECORD

Major updates should record:

- Date
- Information Changed
- Previous Status
- New Status
- Source
- Strategic Impact

---

# 6. CORE RULE

Old information should never be presented as current information.

When current status is uncertain, it should be marked:

REQUIRES UPDATE
