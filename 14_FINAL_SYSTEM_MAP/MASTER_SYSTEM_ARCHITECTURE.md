# ACCESS ALGERIA
# MASTER SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

> The master architecture of the Access Algeria operating system.

---

## 1. SYSTEM PURPOSE

Access Algeria is a strategic market-entry and business-development operating system designed to help international companies evaluate, enter and develop opportunities in Algeria.

The system combines:

- Market Intelligence
- Brand Intelligence
- Opportunity Assessment
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Partnership Intelligence
- Market Entry Strategy
- Operations
- Client Delivery
- Execution
- Knowledge Management
- Performance Management

# 8. HUMAN-AI PRINCIPLE

AI may assist with:

- Research
- Structuring
- Summarization
- Comparison
- Pattern identification
- Scenario analysis
- Drafting
- Knowledge organization

Human authority remains responsible for:

- Strategic decisions
- Commercial commitments
- Legal interpretation
- Institutional commitments
- Negotiation decisions
- Final recommendations

---

# 9. SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Each major information category should have a defined authoritative system.

The repository should avoid uncontrolled duplication.

# 10. SYSTEM PRINCIPLE
