# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PROJECT LIFECYCLE

> Standard lifecycle for Access Algeria market-entry projects.

---

# STAGE 1 — OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY

Identify a potential opportunity.

Output:

Opportunity Record

---

# STAGE 2 — INITIAL SCREENING

Assess whether the opportunity deserves further research.

Output:

Screening Decision

---

# STAGE 3 — INTELLIGENCE

Research:

- Market
- Brand
- Competition
- Regulation
- Partners
- Economics

Output:

Structured Intelligence

---

# STAGE 4 — VERIFICATION

Validate critical claims.

Output:

Verified Intelligence

---

# STAGE 5 — OPPORTUNITY SCORING

Calculate:

- Opportunity Score
- Confidence
- Priority

Output:

Opportunity Decision

---

# STAGE 6 — STRATEGY

Develop:

- Strategic options
- Market-entry models
- Partner strategy
- Risk assessment

Output:

Strategic Recommendation

---

# STAGE 7 — DECISION

Determine whether to:

- Proceed
- Proceed with Conditions
- Research Further
- Pause
- Stop

---

# STAGE 8 — EXECUTION

Transfer approved actions into Monday.

Output:

Execution Plan

---

# STAGE 9 — REVIEW

Measure:

- Progress
- Results
- Risks
- KPIs

---

# STAGE 10 — LEARNING

Document:

- Lessons
- Failed assumptions
- Successful practices
- Methodology improvements

---

# LIFECYCLE LOOP

OPPORTUNITY
→
INTELLIGENCE
→
DECISION
→
EXECUTION
→
RESULT
→
LEARNING
→
NEXT OPPORTUNITY
