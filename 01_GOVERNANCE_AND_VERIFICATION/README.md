# ACCESS ALGERIA
# GOVERNANCE AND VERIFICATION SYSTEM

> A structured framework for protecting strategic decisions from unreliable information, unsupported assumptions and uncontrolled AI-generated outputs.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This system establishes the governance and verification standards used across Access Algeria.

Its purpose is to ensure that strategic decisions, research outputs and recommendations are based on clearly classified and appropriately verified information.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

No important strategic conclusion should be treated as a verified fact without appropriate evidence.

Access Algeria distinguishes between:

- FACT
- SOURCE
- VERIFIED FACT
- ASSUMPTION
- HYPOTHESIS
- ANALYSIS
- RECOMMENDATION

These categories must not be mixed.

---

# 3. INFORMATION HIERARCHY

Information should be evaluated according to its source quality.

## LEVEL 1 — PRIMARY SOURCES

Examples:

- Official company websites
- Official government websites
- Official legislation
- Regulatory authorities
- Official company reports
- Official filings

---

## LEVEL 2 — HIGH-CREDIBILITY SECONDARY SOURCES

Examples:

- Reputable international institutions
- Established research organizations
- Major professional publications

---

## LEVEL 3 — SECONDARY INFORMATION

Examples:

- News articles
- Industry publications
- Market commentary

---

## LEVEL 4 — UNVERIFIED OR LOW-CONFIDENCE INFORMATION

Examples:

- Unconfirmed claims
- Social media claims
- Anonymous statements
- Unsupported AI-generated content

---

# 4. VERIFICATION LEVELS

Every important piece of information should be assigned one of the following statuses:

UNVERIFIED

SOURCED

CROSS-CHECKED

VERIFIED

APPROVED FOR STRATEGIC USE

---

# 5. AI GOVERNANCE RULE

AI tools may assist with:

- Research
- Information discovery
- Data organization
- Analysis
- Strategic synthesis

However:

AI output is not automatically a verified fact.

Any critical information generated or identified by AI should be verified before being used as the basis for a major strategic decision.

---

# 6. SOURCE RECORDING

Important research findings should record:

- Claim
- Source
- Source type
- Publication date
- Date accessed
- Verification status
- Confidence level
- Relevant notes

---

# 7. CONFLICTING INFORMATION

When credible sources provide conflicting information:

1. Do not select the preferred answer without explanation.
2. Record the conflict.
3. Investigate the difference.
4. Prefer primary or more recent authoritative sources where appropriate.
5. Maintain uncertainty where verification is not possible.

---

# 8. STRATEGIC DECISION STANDARD

Major strategic decisions should identify:

- What is known
- What is verified
- What is assumed
- What remains uncertain
- What requires further research

---

# 9. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

The purpose of governance is not to slow down execution.

The purpose is to prevent:

- Poor decisions
- Unverified claims
- Strategic overconfidence
- Repeated research
- Loss of institutional knowledge
