# SOURCE REGISTRY

> Central registry of important sources used for strategic research and decision-making.

---

# SOURCE RECORD STANDARD

Each important source should include:

- Source ID
- Source Name
- Source Type
- Organization
- Publication Date
- Access Date
- URL or Reference
- Related Project
- Related Topic
- Verification Status
- Confidence Level
- Notes

---

## SECONDARY

- Research Organization
- Professional Publication
- Reputable News Source

## OTHER

- Industry Commentary
- Market Discussion
- Preliminary Information
