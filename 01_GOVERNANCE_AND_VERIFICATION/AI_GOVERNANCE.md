# ACCESS ALGERIA
# AI GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines how AI tools may be used within Access Algeria.

AI is used to improve research, analysis, productivity and knowledge management.

AI does not replace verification, professional judgment or strategic accountability.

---

# 2. CORE AI PRINCIPLE

AI OUTPUT
≠
VERIFIED FACT

AI-generated information must be evaluated before strategic use.

---

# 3. PERPLEXITY ROLE

Primary role:

- Research discovery
- Source discovery
- Initial fact-finding
- Market research
- Current information research

---

# 4. CLAUDE ROLE

Primary role:

- Strategic analysis
- Information synthesis
- Framework development
- Document development
- Scenario analysis

---

# 5. GITHUB ROLE

Primary role:

- Knowledge storage
- Documentation
- Version history
- Strategic frameworks
- Institutional memory

---

# 6. MONDAY ROLE

Primary role:

- Task management
- Project execution
- Deadlines
- Follow-ups
- Operational tracking

---

# 7. HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

The final responsibility for:

- Strategic decisions
- External claims
- Partner engagement
- Commercial commitments

remains with the responsible human decision-maker.
