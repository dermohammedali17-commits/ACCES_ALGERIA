# ACCESS ALGERIA
# FACTS VERIFICATION SYSTEM

## 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the standard process used by Access Algeria to verify important information before it is used in research, analysis, strategic decisions or external communications.

---

## 2. CORE RULE

No critical claim should be treated as verified solely because it appears in:

- An AI-generated response
- A news article
- A social media post
- A third-party website
- An unverified document

Important claims require appropriate evidence.

---

## 3. VERIFICATION PROCESS

CLAIM
↓
IDENTIFY SOURCE
↓
CHECK SOURCE QUALITY
↓
CHECK DATE AND CURRENCY
↓
CROSS-CHECK WHEN NECESSARY
↓
CLASSIFY CONFIDENCE
↓
APPROVE OR FLAG FOR FURTHER RESEARCH

---

## 4. INFORMATION CLASSIFICATION

Every important statement should be classified as:

### FACT

A claim supported by reliable evidence.

### VERIFIED FACT

A claim confirmed through appropriate verification.

### ASSUMPTION

A statement considered plausible but not yet confirmed.

### HYPOTHESIS

A proposition that requires testing.

### ANALYSIS

An interpretation of available information.

### RECOMMENDATION

A proposed course of action based on analysis.

---

## 5. VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

Before marking information as verified, check:

- Is the source identifiable?
- Is the source credible?
- Is the information current?
- Is the original source available?
- Has the claim been cross-checked where necessary?
- Does contradictory information exist?

---

## 6. CONFIDENCE LEVELS

HIGH CONFIDENCE

Supported by authoritative or primary sources.

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

Supported by credible evidence but requires additional verification.

LOW CONFIDENCE

Limited, incomplete or indirect evidence.

UNVERIFIED

Insufficient evidence available.

---

## 7. OUTPUT STANDARD

Important research outputs should clearly state:

- Claim
- Evidence
- Source
- Verification Status
- Confidence Level
- Notes

---

## 8. FINAL RULE

When evidence is insufficient, Access Algeria should state:

"Not sufficiently verified."

Uncertainty should be documented rather than hidden.
