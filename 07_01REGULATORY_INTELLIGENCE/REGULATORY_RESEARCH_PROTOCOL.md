# ACCESS ALGERIA
# REGULATORY RESEARCH PROTOCOL

> A standard process for researching regulatory factors affecting market entry.

---

# 1. RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Before researching regulations, define:

- The company or brand
- The sector
- The proposed activity
- The proposed entry model
- The specific regulatory question

---

# 2. RESEARCH PROCESS

## STEP 1 — DEFINE THE QUESTION

Example:

What regulatory conditions may apply to this market-entry model?

---

## STEP 2 — IDENTIFY THE RELEVANT AREA

Possible areas include:

- Investment
- Company formation
- Import
- Distribution
- Licensing
- Sector regulation
- Tax
- Customs

---

## STEP 3 — IDENTIFY AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES

Priority should generally be given to:

1. Official legislation
2. Official government institutions
3. Regulatory authorities
4. Official publications

Secondary sources should be used carefully.

---

## STEP 4 — RECORD THE INFORMATION

Each significant finding should include:

- Regulatory subject
- Summary
- Source
- Publication or update date
- Relevance to the project
- Verification status

---

## STEP 5 — VERIFY

Important information should be checked against authoritative sources.

---

## STEP 6 — ASSESS IMPACT

Assess potential impact on:

- Feasibility
- Timing
- Cost
- Entry model
- Operations
- Risk

---

# 3. CORE RULE

Do not convert uncertain information into a regulatory conclusion.

Use:

- Confirmed
- Under Verification
- Requires Specialist Review

where appropriate.
