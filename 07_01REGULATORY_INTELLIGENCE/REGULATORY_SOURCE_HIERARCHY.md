# ACCESS ALGERIA
# REGULATORY SOURCE HIERARCHY

> A source-priority framework for regulatory intelligence.

---

# LEVEL 1 — PRIMARY AUTHORITY

Highest priority:

- Official legislation
- Official legal publications
- Official government publications
- Official regulatory authorities

---

# LEVEL 2 — OFFICIAL INSTITUTIONAL SOURCES

High priority:

- Ministries
- Government agencies
- Public authorities
- Official investment institutions

---

# LEVEL 3 — PROFESSIONAL INTERPRETATION

Useful for interpretation:

- Qualified legal professionals
- Professional advisory firms
- Recognized industry associations

These sources should not automatically replace primary legal sources.

---

# LEVEL 4 — SECONDARY INFORMATION

Lower priority:

- Media
- General websites
- Commercial summaries
- Unverified online content

These sources may identify a question but should not be the final basis for an important regulatory conclusion.

---

# SOURCE RULE

The higher the impact of a regulatory conclusion, the higher the required source quality.

---

# VERIFICATION RULE

Critical requirements should be checked for:

- Authority
- Current validity
- Date
- Project relevance
