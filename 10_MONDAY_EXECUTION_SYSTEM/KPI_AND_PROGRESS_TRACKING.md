# ACCESS ALGERIA
# KPI AND PROGRESS TRACKING

> A framework for measuring strategic and operational progress.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

KPIs should measure meaningful progress rather than activity volume.

---

# 2. KPI CATEGORIES

## STRATEGIC KPIs

Measure progress toward strategic objectives.

Examples:

- Qualified opportunities
- Active market-entry projects
- Strategic engagements
- Repeat engagements

---

## OPERATIONAL KPIs

Measure execution efficiency.

Examples:

- Task completion rate
- Milestone completion rate
- Overdue critical tasks
- Follow-up completion rate

---

## INTELLIGENCE KPIs

Measure the quality and development of the knowledge system.

Examples:

- Verified intelligence records
- Research completion time
- Source verification rate
- Updated intelligence records

---

## COMMERCIAL KPIs

Measure business development.

Examples:

- Qualified leads
- Active discussions
- Proposals
- Active clients
- Revenue

# 5. CORE PRINCIPLE

A KPI should influence a decision.

Avoid measuring metrics that do not support action or learning.
