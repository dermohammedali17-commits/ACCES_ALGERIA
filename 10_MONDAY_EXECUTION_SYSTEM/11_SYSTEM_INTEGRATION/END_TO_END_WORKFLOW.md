# ACCESS ALGERIA
# END-TO-END WORKFLOW

> The complete workflow for processing a market-entry opportunity.

---

# STEP 1 — DISCOVERY

Identify a potential international company, brand or market opportunity.

↓

# STEP 2 — SCREENING

Determine whether the opportunity deserves further investigation.

↓

# STEP 3 — RESEARCH

Research:

- Brand
- Market
- Competition
- Regulation
- Partners
- Economics

↓

# STEP 4 — VERIFICATION

Validate important information.

↓

# STEP 5 — INTELLIGENCE STORAGE

Store verified knowledge in GitHub.

↓

# STEP 6 — OPPORTUNITY SCORING

Evaluate the opportunity using the Access Algeria scoring framework.

↓

# STEP 7 — STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

Analyze:

- Opportunity
- Risks
- Scenarios
- Feasibility

↓

# STEP 8 — MARKET ENTRY DESIGN

Develop potential market-entry approaches.

↓

# STEP 9 — PARTNER STRATEGY

Identify and assess potential partners.

↓

# STEP 10 — DECISION

Decide:

- Proceed
- Proceed with Conditions
- Research Further
- Pause
- Stop

↓

# STEP 11 — EXECUTION

Approved actions are transferred to Monday.

↓

# STEP 12 — MEASUREMENT

Track:

- Milestones
- KPIs
- Results
- Risks

↓

# STEP 13 — LEARNING

Capture lessons learned.

↓

# STEP 14 — SYSTEM UPDATE

Update relevant:

- Intelligence
- Methodology
- Templates
- Scoring
- Knowledge
