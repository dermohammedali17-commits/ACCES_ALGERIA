# ACCESS ALGERIA
# MONDAY OPERATING PROTOCOL

## 1. PURPOSE

Monday functions as the execution and operational tracking layer of Access Algeria.

---

## 2. MONDAY SHOULD TRACK

- Opportunities
- Projects
- Tasks
- Stakeholders
- Follow-ups
- Milestones
- Deadlines
- Owners
- Risks
- Decisions
- Deliverables

---

## 3. MONDAY SHOULD NOT BECOME

Monday should not replace the controlled strategic knowledge repository.

Strategic methodologies, verified intelligence and governance documents remain controlled within GitHub.

---

## 4. EXECUTION FLOW

APPROVED DECISION
↓
ACTION
↓
OWNER
↓
DEADLINE
↓
EXECUTION
↓
STATUS
↓
RESULT
↓
REVIEW

---

## 5. REQUIRED ITEM INFORMATION

Where applicable:

- Project
- Opportunity
- Task
- Owner
- Status
- Priority
- Deadline
- Stakeholder
- Related decision
- Related repository document
- Next action
- Result

---

## 6. STATUS MODEL

Possible statuses:

- BACKLOG
- PLANNED
- IN PROGRESS
- WAITING
- BLOCKED
- COMPLETED
- CANCELLED
- ARCHIVED

---

## 7. GITHUB RELATIONSHIP

GitHub defines the system and strategic knowledge.

Monday manages execution.

A Monday item should reference the relevant project, decision or repository document where appropriate.

---

## 8. REVIEW

Execution data should feed:

- Performance Management
- Knowledge Management
- Lessons Learned
- Strategic Review

---

## 9. CORE PRINCIPLE

Monday answers:

"What needs to happen, who owns it, and when?"

GitHub answers:

"What is the system, what do we know, and why are we doing it?"
