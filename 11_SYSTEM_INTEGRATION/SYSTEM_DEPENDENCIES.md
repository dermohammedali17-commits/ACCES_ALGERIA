# ACCESS ALGERIA
# SYSTEM DEPENDENCIES

> Defines the relationship and dependencies between the major Access Algeria systems.

---

## 1. MASTER CONTROL

All major systems must align with:

- Company Identity
- Strategic Objectives
- Business Model
- Project Scope

---

## 2. GOVERNANCE

All intelligence and strategic analysis must follow:

- Verification Standards
- Source Standards
- Assumption Management
- AI Governance

---

## 3. MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Supports:

- Brand Assessment
- Opportunity Scoring
- Market Entry Strategy

---

## 4. BRAND DATABASE

Supports:

- Brand Intelligence
- Contact Intelligence
- Opportunity Discovery

---

## 5. OPPORTUNITY SCORING

Depends on:

- Market Intelligence
- Brand Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Partnership Intelligence

---

## 6. MARKET ENTRY

Depends on:

- Opportunity Assessment
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Partner Strategy
- Market Intelligence

---

## 7. EXECUTION

Depends on:

- Approved Strategy
- Strategic Decisions
- Implementation Roadmap

---

## 8. PORTFOLIO

Depends on:

- Active Opportunities
- Projects
- Decisions
- Execution Status
- KPIs

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

A system should not duplicate another system's function.

Each system should contribute a specific capability to the overall architecture.
