# ACCESS ALGERIA
# INTEGRATION RULES

---

## RULE 1 — SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Important information should have one primary location.

Other systems should reference it instead of duplicating it.

---

## RULE 2 — VERIFY BEFORE STRATEGY

Unverified information should not be treated as confirmed strategic evidence.

---

## RULE 3 — STRATEGY BEFORE EXECUTION

Major execution should follow a documented strategic decision.

---

## RULE 4 — RECORD DECISIONS

Important decisions should be recorded with:

- Context
- Evidence
- Rationale
- Consequences

---

## RULE 5 — CONNECT ACTIONS TO OBJECTIVES

Every significant project or task should support:

- A strategic objective
- A project
- Or a documented decision

---

## RULE 6 — KEEP SYSTEMS SPECIALIZED

Each system should maintain its defined function.

Avoid unnecessary duplication.

---

## RULE 7 — UPDATE WHEN REALITY CHANGES

The system must adapt when:

- Market conditions change
- Regulations change
- New evidence appears
- Strategic assumptions fail

---

## RULE 8 — HUMAN DECISION AUTHORITY

AI may assist with:

- Research
- Analysis
- Structuring
- Scenario development

Final strategic and commercial decisions remain under human responsibility.
