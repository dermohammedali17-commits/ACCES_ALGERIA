# ACCESS ALGERIA
# INFORMATION FLOW

> Defines the lifecycle of information inside Access Algeria.

---

## 1. DISCOVERY

Information may originate from:

- Official Sources
- Company Sources
- Market Research
- Industry Sources
- Strategic Partners
- Direct Communication
- AI-Assisted Research

---

## 2. COLLECTION

Information is collected and recorded.

Raw information should not automatically become a strategic conclusion.

---

## 3. VERIFICATION

Critical information should be:

- Checked
- Compared
- Sourced
- Classified

---

## 4. STORAGE

Verified information should be stored in the relevant system.

Examples:

Market Data
→ Market Intelligence

Brand Information
→ Brand Database

Partner Information
→ Partnership Intelligence

Regulatory Information
→ Regulatory Intelligence

---

## 5. ANALYSIS

Stored intelligence may be used for:

- Market Assessment
- Opportunity Scoring
- Partner Assessment
- Risk Analysis
- Market Entry Strategy

---

## 6. DECISION

Important decisions should be recorded in:

Decision Register

---

## 7. ACTION

Approved decisions should be converted into:

- Projects
- Tasks
- Milestones
- KPIs

---

## 8. FEEDBACK

Results should improve future decisions.

---

## CORE FLOW

RAW INFORMATION
↓
VERIFICATION
↓
STRUCTURED INTELLIGENCE
↓

↓

↓
ACTION
