# ACCESS ALGERIA
# CORE METHODOLOGY

> A structured architecture for transforming market intelligence into market-entry opportunities.

---

# 1. METHODOLOGY PURPOSE

The Access Algeria Core Methodology defines the standard process used to identify, evaluate, structure and support market-entry opportunities.

Its purpose is to ensure that opportunities are not pursued based solely on intuition, personal relationships or isolated market information.

Every significant opportunity should move through a structured decision process.

---

# STAGE 0 — DISCOVERY

## Objective

Identify potential international brands, sectors or market-entry opportunities.

## Key Questions

- What opportunity has been identified?
- Which company or brand is involved?
- Why might Algeria be relevant?
- What market gap or strategic signal has been observed?
- Is there sufficient reason to investigate further?

## Inputs

- Market observations
- Industry trends
- Brand expansion signals
- Company announcements
- Market gaps
- Professional networks
- Strategic partnerships
- Economic and regulatory signals

## Output

Opportunity Record

## Decision

- Research Candidate
- Watchlist
- Rejected

# 3. DECISION GATES

Every major opportunity should pass through defined decision gates.

## Gate 1 — Research Decision

Should we investigate?

YES / NO

## Gate 2 — Opportunity Decision

Is this opportunity strategically attractive?

PROCEED / WATCHLIST / STOP

## Gate 3 — Entry Decision

Should a market-entry strategy be developed?

YES / CONDITIONAL / NO

## Gate 4 — Engagement Decision

Should Access Algeria engage the target company or potential partners?

YES / PREPARE MORE / NO

## Gate 5 — Execution Decision

Are the conditions sufficient to proceed?

PROCEED / PAUSE / STOP

---

# 4. MINIMUM VIABLE PROJECT PRINCIPLE

Not every opportunity requires a full-scale project.

Access Algeria should first determine the minimum work necessary to make a reliable next decision.

The principle is:

> Do not build more analysis than the decision requires.

A small opportunity may require:

- Screening
- Basic research
- Initial scoring

A major opportunity may require:

- Full market research
- Regulatory assessment
- Partner mapping
- Due diligence
- Financial analysis
- Market-entry strategy

---

# 5. METHODOLOGY RULE

No stage automatically guarantees progression to the next stage.

The system must allow an opportunity to:

- PROCEED
- PAUSE
- RETURN FOR RESEARCH
- MOVE TO WATCHLIST
- STOP
