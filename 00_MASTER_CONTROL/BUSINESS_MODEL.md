# ACCESS ALGERIA

# BUSINESS MODEL

> The Gateway for Global Brands to Algeria

---

## 1. BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

Access Algeria is designed as an intelligence-led B2B market-entry and business-development platform.

The business model is based on creating value for international companies by reducing market-entry uncertainty and helping transform potential opportunities into structured commercial pathways.

The platform does not depend on a single source of revenue.

Its long-term model combines:

* Strategic advisory
* Market intelligence
* Opportunity assessment
* Market-entry projects
* Business development support
* Partnership development
* Project-based commercial arrangements, where legally and contractually appropriate

---

## 2. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

### Primary Customers

* International manufacturers
* Global brands
* Automotive brands
* Luxury and premium brands
* Tourism and hospitality companies
* International companies evaluating Algeria as a potential market

### Secondary Customers

* Algerian companies seeking international strategic partnerships
* Local investors seeking access to international business opportunities
* Strategic partners participating in market-entry projects

---

## 3. THE CUSTOMER PROBLEM

A company considering Algeria may face:

* Limited market knowledge
* Difficulty assessing genuine demand
* Regulatory uncertainty
* Difficulty identifying credible partners
* Limited local networks
* High information asymmetry
* Uncertainty regarding entry costs and complexity
* Difficulty converting market interest into execution

Access Algeria aims to reduce these barriers.

## 6. REVENUE ARCHITECTURE

Access Algeria may use several revenue mechanisms, depending on the project, applicable law and contractual structure.

### A. RESEARCH OR INTELLIGENCE FEE

A client pays for a defined research or intelligence assignment.

Example:

Market assessment for a specific brand or sector.

---

### B. STRATEGIC ADVISORY FEE

A client pays for strategic analysis and recommendations.

Example:

Market-entry strategy development.

---

### C. PROJECT FEE

A fixed fee is agreed for a defined market-entry project.

Example:

Market assessment through partner strategy.

---

### D. RETAINER

A recurring fee for ongoing strategic or business-development support.

Example:

Monthly or quarterly market-development support.

---

### E. MILESTONE-BASED COMMERCIAL MODEL

Payments are linked to defined deliverables or milestones.

Example:

* Research completed
* Market assessment completed
* Partner mapping completed
* Strategy approved

---

### F. SUCCESS-BASED COMMERCIAL ARRANGEMENT

Where legally, commercially and contractually appropriate, compensation may be linked to a defined successful commercial outcome.

This structure should always include:

* Clear contractual definitions
* Measurable success criteria
* Compliance review
* Appropriate legal documentation
