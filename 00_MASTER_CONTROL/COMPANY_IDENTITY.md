## 1. CORE IDENTITY

**Name:** Access Algeria

**Strategic Positioning:** Strategic Market Entry & Business Development Platform

**Primary Market:** Algeria

**Primary Client Base:** International companies and brands evaluating or pursuing opportunities in the Algerian market.

**Core Orientation:** B2B

**Operating Nature:** Intelligence-led, evidence-driven and partnership-oriented.

---

## 2. WHO WE ARE

Access Algeria is a strategic platform designed to help international brands understand, evaluate and approach the Algerian market.

The platform combines local market understanding with structured research, market intelligence, strategic analysis and business-development capabilities.

Access Algeria aims to reduce the information gap and execution gap between international companies and the Algerian business environment.
