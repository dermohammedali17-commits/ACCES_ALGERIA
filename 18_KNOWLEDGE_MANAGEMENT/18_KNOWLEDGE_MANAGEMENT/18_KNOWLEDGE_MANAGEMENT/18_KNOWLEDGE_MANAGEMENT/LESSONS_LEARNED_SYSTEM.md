# LESSONS LEARNED SYSTEM

## 1. PURPOSE

The Lessons Learned System converts experience into institutional knowledge and continuous improvement.

---

## 2. WHEN TO CAPTURE A LESSON

Lessons should be captured after:

- Major meetings
- Negotiations
- Important stakeholder interactions
- Failed approaches
- Successful approaches
- Project milestones
- Project completion
- Strategic decisions
- Significant unexpected events

---

## 3. LESSON RECORD

Each lesson should capture:

- Date
- Project
- Context
- Objective
- What happened
- Expected result
- Actual result
- Variance
- Cause
- Lesson
- Recommended change
- Owner
- Follow-up date

---

## 4. LESSON TYPES

- Strategic
- Commercial
- Regulatory
- Operational
- Partnership
- Research
- Communication
- Negotiation
- Execution

---

## 5. LESSON → SYSTEM UPDATE

A lesson may result in changes to:

- Methodology
- Workflow
- Template
- Scoring model
- Research protocol
- Stakeholder strategy
- Market-entry strategy
- KPI framework
- Decision criteria

---

## 6. REVIEW

Lessons should periodically be reviewed to identify recurring patterns.

Repeated lessons may indicate a systemic weakness rather than an isolated incident.

---

## 7. CORE PRINCIPLE

Access Algeria should not repeatedly learn the same lesson.

Experience becomes strategically valuable when it changes future behavior.
