# KNOWLEDGE RETENTION AND UPDATE

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines when Access Algeria knowledge should be retained, reviewed, updated or archived.

---

## 2. RETAIN

Retain information when it has:

- Strategic value
- Operational value
- Legal or regulatory relevance
- Historical significance
- Relationship value
- Reusable research value
- Decision-making relevance

---

## 3. UPDATE

Information should be reviewed when:

- Regulations change
- Market conditions change
- Company information changes
- Stakeholder roles change
- Strategic assumptions change
- A major project milestone occurs
- New evidence materially changes an existing conclusion

---

## 4. ARCHIVE

Archive information when:

- It is no longer operationally relevant
- It has been superseded
- A project has closed
- A strategic direction has been discontinued
- Historical preservation remains useful

---

## 5. DO NOT DELETE WITHOUT REVIEW

Potentially important information should not be permanently deleted simply because it appears outdated.

First determine whether it should be:

- Updated
- Superseded
- Archived
- Merged
- Reclassified

---

## 6. VERSION CONTROL

Material changes should preserve:

- Previous version
- New version
- Date
- Reason for change
- Change owner

---

## 7. KNOWLEDGE REVIEW CYCLE

### Weekly

Operational knowledge and active research.

### Monthly

Important market, stakeholder and project information.

### Quarterly

Strategic assumptions and major knowledge assets.

### Event-Driven

Immediate review following major regulatory, market or strategic changes.

---

## 8. SOURCE OF TRUTH

Each important knowledge item should have one authoritative location.

Other systems should reference that source instead of creating unnecessary copies.
