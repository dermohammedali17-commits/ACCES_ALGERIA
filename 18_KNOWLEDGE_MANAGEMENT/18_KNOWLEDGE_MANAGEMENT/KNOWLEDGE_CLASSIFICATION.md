# KNOWLEDGE CLASSIFICATION

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines how Access Algeria classifies information and knowledge.

---

## 2. FACT

Verified information supported by reliable evidence.

Examples:

- Official regulatory information
- Confirmed company information
- Verified market data
- Confirmed stakeholder information

---

## 3. ASSUMPTION

An input that has not yet been sufficiently verified.

Assumptions must not be presented as established facts.

---

## 4. ANALYSIS

Interpretation derived from available evidence.

Analysis should identify the evidence on which it is based.

---

## 5. INSIGHT

A strategically meaningful conclusion derived from multiple pieces of information.

---

## 6. DECISION

A documented strategic or operational choice.

Decisions should include:

- Context
- Evidence
- Options
- Rationale
- Risks
- Owner
- Next action

---

## 7. LESSON

Knowledge generated from an actual result, failure, success or experience.

---

## 8. ARCHIVED KNOWLEDGE

Information that is no longer operationally current but remains valuable for historical reference.

---

## 9. CONFIDENCE

Where appropriate, information should be classified as:

- HIGH CONFIDENCE
- MEDIUM CONFIDENCE
- LOW CONFIDENCE
- UNVERIFIED

---

## 10. CORE RULE

Information must never gain authority simply because it appears repeatedly in the repository.

Authority comes from evidence, verification and appropriate source quality.
