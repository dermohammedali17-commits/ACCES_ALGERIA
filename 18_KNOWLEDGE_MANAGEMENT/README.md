# ACCESS ALGERIA
# KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

> The system responsible for capturing, organizing, maintaining and reusing Access Algeria knowledge.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Knowledge Management System ensures that important information, research, decisions, lessons and institutional knowledge are captured and reusable.

---

## 2. KNOWLEDGE CATEGORIES

- Market Knowledge
- Brand Knowledge
- Regulatory Knowledge
- Partnership Knowledge
- Stakeholder Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- Client Knowledge
- Strategic Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Lessons Learned

---

## 4. KNOWLEDGE PRINCIPLES

- Capture important knowledge at the source.
- Distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Record sources whenever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary duplication.
- Maintain clear ownership.
- Update information when material changes occur.
- Preserve important historical decisions.
- Make knowledge reusable across projects.
