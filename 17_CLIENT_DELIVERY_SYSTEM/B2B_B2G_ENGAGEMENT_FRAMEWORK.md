# B2B AND B2G ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines how Access Algeria approaches commercial and institutional stakeholders.

---

# 2. B2B ENGAGEMENT

## Target Stakeholders

- International brands
- Manufacturers
- Local companies
- Distributors
- Dealers
- Investors
- Financial institutions
- Strategic partners
- Service providers

IDENTIFY
↓

↓
RESEARCH
↓
STRATEGIC FIT
↓
CONTACT
↓
ENGAGE
↓
PROPOSE
↓

↓

↓

# 3. B2G ENGAGEMENT

## Target Stakeholders

---

# 4. B2B VS B2G PRINCIPLE

B2B engagement is primarily commercial and partnership-oriented.

B2G engagement is institutional and procedure-oriented.

Access Algeria must not treat government institutions as ordinary commercial prospects.

---

# 5. ENGAGEMENT RECORD

Every significant engagement should record:

- Organization
- Stakeholder
- Position
- Stakeholder type
- B2B / B2G
- Objective
- Date
- Communication channel
- Discussion summary
- Evidence
- Outcome
- Next action
- Responsible person
- Follow-up date
- Status

---

# 6. COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES

All engagement should be:

- Professional
- Evidence-based
- Accurate
- Respectful of institutional procedures
- Clear about Access Algeria's role
- Free from unsupported claims
- Properly documented

---

# 7. ESCALATION

Escalate when:

- Decision authority is unclear
- Regulatory interpretation is uncertain
- Commercial commitments are requested
- Legal implications arise
- Material financial commitments are involved
- Strategic risk becomes significant

---

# 8. SYSTEM CONNECTIONS

Engagement intelligence should feed:

- Partnership Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Market Intelligence
- Opportunity Scoring
- Operations
- Monday Execution System
- Portfolio and Dashboard
