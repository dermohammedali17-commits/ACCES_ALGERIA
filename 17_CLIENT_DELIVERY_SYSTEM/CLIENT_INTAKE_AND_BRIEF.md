# CLIENT INTAKE AND BRIEF

## 1. PURPOSE

The Client Intake and Brief process captures the information required to understand a client's objectives, constraints and expected outcomes before work begins.

---

## 2. CLIENT INFORMATION

- Client Name
- Company
- Country
- Industry
- Website
- Contact Person
- Position
- Contact Information
- Client Type
- B2B / B2G
- Date of Engagement

---

## 3. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

Document:

- Primary objective
- Secondary objectives
- Algeria-related motivation
- Expected business outcome
- Strategic importance
- Target timeline

---

## 4. MARKET ENTRY OBJECTIVE

Identify whether the client is seeking:

- Market assessment
- Market entry
- Local partner
- Distributor
- Dealer
- Investor
- Joint venture
- Government engagement
- Regulatory understanding
- Commercial development
- Strategic representation
- Other

---

## 5. SCOPE

Define:

- Geographic scope
- Sector scope
- Product scope
- Target customer
- Required research
- Required stakeholders
- Expected deliverables
- Timeline
- Dependencies

---

## 6. CONSTRAINTS

Record:

- Budget constraints
- Timeline constraints
- Regulatory constraints
- Commercial constraints
- Internal client limitations
- Confidentiality requirements
- Strategic restrictions

---

## 7. SUCCESS CRITERIA

Define measurable outcomes before execution.

Examples:

- Qualified partners identified
- Regulatory pathway clarified
- Market opportunity assessed
- Stakeholder meetings secured
- Market-entry options evaluated
- Strategic recommendation delivered

---

## 8. INITIAL RISK SCREENING

Evaluate:

- Strategic fit
- Regulatory complexity
- Commercial feasibility
- Partner availability
- Reputation risk
- Execution complexity
- Information gaps

---

## 9. INTERNAL DECISION

Possible outcomes:

- ACCEPT
- ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS
- REQUEST MORE INFORMATION
- DEFER
- DECLINE

---

## 10. SYSTEM CONNECTIONS

Client information should connect to:

- Opportunity Scoring
- Market Intelligence
- Brand Database
- Partnership Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Market Entry
- Operations
- Monday Execution System
