# MARKET ENTRY DELIVERABLE FRAMEWORK

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines the standard structure for Access Algeria market-entry deliverables.

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Every major deliverable should begin with:

- Client objective
- Key market findings
- Strategic opportunity
- Major constraints
- Key risks
- Recommended direction
- Immediate next actions

---

## 3. MARKET ANALYSIS

Where relevant, evaluate:

- Market size
- Market growth
- Demand
- Customer segments
- Competitive landscape
- Industry structure
- Distribution structure
- Pricing environment
- Market trends

---

## 4. REGULATORY ANALYSIS

Evaluate:

- Applicable laws
- Regulations
- Executive decrees
- Administrative requirements
- Licensing
- Import requirements
- Investment requirements
- Tax considerations
- Sector-specific requirements

All regulatory conclusions must be verified through appropriate authoritative sources.

---

## 5. PARTNER ANALYSIS

Potential partners should be evaluated according to:

- Strategic fit
- Financial capability
- Market presence
- Reputation
- Infrastructure
- Distribution capability
- Management capability
- Relevant relationships
- Track record
- Risk profile

---

## 6. ENTRY OPTIONS

Potential entry models may include:

- Local distributor
- Dealer
- Commercial representation
- Joint venture
- Strategic partnership
- Local subsidiary
- Investment project
- Government-supported project
- Other legally appropriate structures

---

## 7. OPTION COMPARISON

Each option should be evaluated using:

- Strategic fit
- Investment requirement
- Regulatory complexity
- Time to market
- Operational complexity
- Risk
- Scalability
- Control
- Expected commercial potential

---

## 8. RECOMMENDATION

The recommendation should clearly state:

- Preferred option
- Rationale
- Evidence supporting the recommendation
- Major assumptions
- Key risks
- Mitigation measures
- Required decisions

---

## 9. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

Define:

### Phase 1
Preparation

### Phase 2
Validation

### Phase 3
Stakeholder Engagement

### Phase 4
Negotiation

### Phase 5
Market Entry Preparation

### Phase 6
Execution

### Phase 7
Performance Review

---

## 10. DECISION STANDARD

Recommendations must distinguish between:

**FACT**

Verified information.

**ASSUMPTION**

Information requiring validation.

**ANALYSIS**

Interpretation derived from evidence.

**RECOMMENDATION**

Strategic conclusion based on available evidence.

**UNCERTAINTY**

Material information that remains unresolved.
