# EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD FRAMEWORK

## 1. PURPOSE

The Executive Dashboard provides a concise view of Access Algeria's strategic position, active opportunities, projects, relationships and performance.

---

## 2. EXECUTIVE SECTIONS

### Strategic Position

- Current strategic priorities
- Major objectives
- Strategic risks
- Major decisions pending

### Opportunity Pipeline

- Total opportunities
- Qualified opportunities
- Active opportunities
- Watchlist
- Paused
- Closed

### Client Portfolio

- Active clients
- Prospective clients
- Active mandates
- Deliverables in progress

### Partnership Pipeline

- International brands
- Local partners
- Investors
- Strategic institutions
- B2B relationships
- B2G relationships

### Project Portfolio

- Active projects
- On-track projects
- At-risk projects
- Delayed projects
- Completed projects

### Performance

- Strategic KPIs
- Commercial KPIs
- Operational KPIs
- Project KPIs

---

## 3. RISK VIEW

The dashboard should highlight:

- High-priority risks
- Regulatory risks
- Commercial risks
- Partnership risks
- Execution risks
- Information gaps

---

## 4. DECISION VIEW

Display important decisions requiring attention:

- Decision
- Context
- Deadline
- Owner
- Required evidence
- Current status

---

## 5. DASHBOARD PRINCIPLE

The dashboard should provide decision-relevant information rather than reproduce the entire repository.

---
