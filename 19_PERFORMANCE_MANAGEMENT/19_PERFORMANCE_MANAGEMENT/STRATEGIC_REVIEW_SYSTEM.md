# STRATEGIC REVIEW SYSTEM

## 1. PURPOSE

The Strategic Review System ensures that Access Algeria periodically evaluates whether its strategy remains valid and aligned with market reality.

---

## 2. REVIEW QUESTIONS

Every strategic review should examine:

### Direction

Are we pursuing the right objectives?

### Market

Has the external environment changed?

### Opportunities

Are priority opportunities still attractive?

### Clients

Are client needs changing?

### Partnerships

Are existing relationships producing strategic value?

### Regulation

Have regulatory conditions changed?

### Resources

Are available resources sufficient?

### Execution

Are strategic initiatives progressing?

### Risk

Have material risks changed?

---

## 5. REVIEW TRIGGERS

A strategic review should occur:

- Quarterly
- After major market changes
- After major regulatory changes
- Following major project outcomes
- Following major partnership developments
- When assumptions materially change

---

## 6. DOCUMENTATION

Every major strategic review should record:

- Date
- Objectives
- Evidence
- Performance
- Major changes
- Risks
- Options
- Decision
- Rationale
- Actions
- Owner
- Review date

---

## 7. CORE PRINCIPLE

Strategy is not static.

Access Algeria should adapt its strategy when credible evidence demonstrates that assumptions or market conditions have materially changed.
