# ACCESS ALGERIA
# PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

> The system responsible for measuring strategic, commercial and operational performance across Access Algeria.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Performance Management System measures whether Access Algeria is achieving its strategic objectives and whether its operating systems are producing meaningful outcomes.

---

## 2. PERFORMANCE LEVELS

Performance should be evaluated at four levels:

### Strategic

Are Access Algeria's strategic objectives progressing?

### Commercial

Are opportunities, clients and partnerships generating meaningful potential?

### Operational

Are workflows being executed effectively?

### Project

Are individual projects progressing toward defined outcomes?
