# KPI AND TARGET FRAMEWORK

## 1. PURPOSE

This framework defines how Access Algeria establishes, measures and reviews performance indicators.

---

## 2. KPI CATEGORIES

### Strategic KPIs

Examples:

- Strategic objectives achieved
- Strategic initiatives completed
- Priority markets developed
- Strategic partnerships established

### Commercial KPIs

Examples:

- Qualified opportunities
- Active client engagements
- Qualified international brands
- Partner opportunities
- Commercial proposals
- Strategic mandates

### B2B KPIs

Examples:

- International companies contacted
- Qualified B2B stakeholders
- Meetings
- Active negotiations
- Partnerships progressed

### B2G KPIs

Examples:

- Relevant institutions mapped
- Institutional engagements
- Administrative pathways clarified
- Government-related initiatives progressed

### Operational KPIs

Examples:

- Research requests completed
- Verification completion rate
- Workflow completion
- Follow-up completion
- Overdue actions

### Project KPIs

Examples:

- Milestones completed
- Milestones delayed
- Budget performance
- Risk status
- Project objective completion

---

## 3. KPI DEFINITION

Every KPI should define:

- Name
- Purpose
- Formula
- Data source
- Owner
- Frequency
- Target
- Threshold
- Review action

---

## 4. TARGET TYPES

Targets may be:

- Numeric
- Percentage
- Time-based
- Milestone-based
- Quality-based
- Outcome-based

---

## 5. KPI QUALITY RULE

A KPI should exist only when it supports a meaningful decision.

Avoid measuring metrics simply because the data is easy to collect.

---

## 6. REVIEW FREQUENCY

### Weekly

Operational indicators.

### Monthly

Commercial and project indicators.

### Quarterly

Strategic performance.
